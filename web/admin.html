Players

Players are jersey colour + number only. Every player gets a primary and a secondary jersey colour at registration; the number is the same on both. A player's code is built from the primary colour (White 14 = W-14). Within one jersey set, use colours with different first letters. Pick a colour from the list or choose Other… to add a new one. Never paste a roster with names.

Position, primary colour and secondary colour can be changed in the table (they save immediately). Changing the primary colour changes the player's code (W-433 becomes R-433) and is only possible while they have no scores; teams and sessions are updated to the new code. Delete only works for players with no scores yet; use Release once scoring has started. Tick AA for players still being considered for the AA team: evaluators see the badge on that player's card.

Teams

Teams are groups of players you reuse across sessions: the two teams in a scrimmage or game, or the group that is on the ice for a skills session.

  1. Create a team below: give it a name and, for a scrimmage team, its jersey colour. A skills group needs no colour: leave it as None (default jerseys), since red and white are on the ice together on skills nights.
  2. Open the team row (the ▾ chevron or the players · pick pill) and tick the players on it by number. A player can be on more than one team.
  3. On the team row you can rename it, change its colour, or Delete team.
  4. In Sessions below, put two teams on each scrimmage or game with + team (the colour comes from the team), or put the group that is skating on a skills session with + group. Leave a skills group's colour as own colours: in skills sessions players wear their default jersey from the Players list.

Every player on a team wears the team colour, whatever their default jersey: White 433 on a Red team shows to evaluators as R-433. Players not on a team or group that is on the ice are not dressed for that session: evaluators don't see them and cannot score them.

Sessions (ice times)

One row per ice time. Add a session with the form below, then edit it in the table: label, date and type save as soon as you leave the field. Sessions are listed by date, and evaluators pick the session they are scoring from a dropdown that defaults to today's.

  1. Skills sessions: one group on the ice. If you split the players into groups, make each group a team in Teams and put the one that is skating on the session with + group, leaving the colour as default jerseys: players wear their default jersey from the Players list. Once a group is on, the picker goes away; remove it with ✕ to choose another. With no group, everyone is on the ice.
  2. Scrimmages and games: full ice, exactly two teams. In Teams on the ice pick a team and press + team, then the second one. The colour comes from the team; change it here only if that team wears something else for that one session. Players not on a team or group that is on the ice are not dressed and are hidden from evaluators. Remove one with its ✕ to swap it.
  3. Default jerseys only matters when nothing else is set: it picks each player's primary or secondary colour as the fallback. Colours set in Attendance & jerseys win over team colours, which win over this default.

Evaluators always see the colour worn that session with the player's number; scores stay with the player whatever they wear.

Attendance & jerseys

Pick a session, then set two things for that ice time only:

  1. Attendance: untick anyone who is not on the ice (injured, sick). They disappear from every evaluator's screen for this session, cannot be scored for it, and it does not count in their Sessions column. Tick them again if they turn up.
  2. Jerseys: the dropdown on each player is the colour they are wearing today. For a skills night use the bulk row: all forwards → White, Apply, then all defence → Red, Apply. In a scrimmage the list is grouped by team and already shows the team colours; change a player here only if they are wearing something else (a borrowed jersey). Reset to default jerseys clears every colour set here for this session.

Players not on one of a scrimmage's two teams are listed as not dressed and are hidden from evaluators. Evaluators see exactly the colours set here with the player's number; scores always stay with the player. A warning appears if two players would show as the same code.

Overall = weighted average of the criteria that were scored, averaged across evaluations. Spread = standard deviation of each evaluator's overall for that player; rows at or above are highlighted as disagreement worth a second look. Tap a column heading to sort.

Evaluators

Evaluators sign in with their email and a one-time code; there is no password to send. Use a label like "Evaluator 3" — the label is what you see in the rankings, so keep it neutral if you prefer blind evaluators. A login only works on a tryout it has been added to; use Disable to cut someone off without deleting them or their scores.

Close the tryout

Closing stops all scoring. Evaluators can still open the app and see their own scores, read-only. This cannot be undone from the app.
